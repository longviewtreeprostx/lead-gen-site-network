Our coverage area runs roughly 20–25 miles out from Longview in every direction, which puts Kilgore, White Oak, Gladewater, and Hallsville comfortably inside it, along with the smaller communities in between. If you're close to that radius and not sure whether you're in range, the honest answer is just to call and ask — we'll tell you straight rather than send a crew out on a job that doesn't make sense for either of us.

Towns we serve

Longview

Home base, and where most of our work happens day to day. Longview's mix of mature residential neighborhoods and newer subdivisions means we see everything from large, decades-old oaks and pines that have outgrown their space to storm cleanup after the East Texas thunderstorms that roll through every spring and summer.

Kilgore

About 12 miles east/southeast of Longview. We handle both residential removals and the occasional commercial job for Kilgore's downtown and college-area properties, where tight lots and street trees mean careful rigging matters more than raw speed.

White Oak

Just north of Longview, close enough that it's often the same trip as a Longview job. Mostly residential work here — trimming and removal on established neighborhood lots.

Gladewater

Northwest of Longview. We cover both in-town residential jobs and the larger rural properties on Gladewater's outskirts, where land clearing and bigger tree removals are more common than in denser parts of the service area.

Hallsville

East of Longview off Highway 80. A newer, faster-growing community with a lot of recently built homes on larger lots — we see more land-clearing and lot-prep calls here than in the older, more established towns.

Not seeing your town?

We also take jobs throughout the rest of Gregg and Upshur County and nearby communities that fall within our normal drive radius. If your address is anywhere near the towns above, call and we'll tell you whether it's in range — no guessing required.

What we do, wherever you're located

Every service on this site — tree removal, emergency & storm damage response, trimming & pruning, stump grinding, and land clearing — is available anywhere in our coverage area, not just in Longview proper. Response time can vary a bit by distance, especially for non-emergency work, but the crew and equipment showing up is the same either way.
