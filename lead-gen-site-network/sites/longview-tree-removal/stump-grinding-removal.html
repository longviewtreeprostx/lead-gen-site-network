We offer stump grinding as a standalone service or bundled with tree removal, so you're not left with a hole in your yard and a reminder of the tree that used to be there.

Grinding vs. full removal

Grinding is the faster, more affordable option — we grind the stump down several inches below grade so it can be covered with soil and grass grows back over it, though larger roots stay in the ground. Full stump and root removal is more involved (and more expensive), typically only necessary if you're planning new construction, a pool, or landscaping directly where the stump was. For most homeowners, grinding is the right call.

What happens to the grindings

We can leave the wood chips on-site (useful as mulch) or haul them away — your choice, just let us know.

Timing

Stump grinding can typically happen the same day as removal or any time after — there's no rush, though grinding sooner makes it easier to get grass established before the next season.

Bundle and save

Ask about bundling stump grinding with your tree removal for a better combined price than booking separately.
