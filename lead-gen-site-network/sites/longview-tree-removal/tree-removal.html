How pricing works

Tree removal cost depends mainly on the tree's size and species, how accessible it is (open yard vs. tight space, near a structure or power line), and whether it's a routine removal or a hazardous/storm situation requiring extra rigging and care. We'll walk the property with you, explain what we're seeing, and give you a firm number before we start — not a range that changes once the crew shows up.

Dead Tree Removal

A dead tree is not a "wait and see" situation — dead wood is unpredictable and can fail without warning, especially in wind or after a hard rain. If a tree on your property has stopped leafing out, has significant dead limbs, or shows fungus/rot at the base, it's worth a look before storm season, not after a limb comes down.

Large Tree Removal

Big trees need more than a bigger chainsaw — they need a real plan: rigging to control where sections fall, the right equipment for the size and location, and a crew that's done this before. We handle large hazardous removals routinely, including trees close to homes, fences, and power lines.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Longview?

Requirements can depend on your specific property, the tree, and the situation. Give us a call and we'll help you figure out what, if anything, applies before any work begins.

How long does a typical removal take?

Most single-tree removals are completed in a few hours to a full day, depending on size and access.

What happens to the wood?

We haul it away as part of standard service; if you'd like to keep firewood-length rounds, just ask — we're happy to leave them.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes, as an add-on — see our Stump Grinding page.

Is someone going to be on my roof or near my power lines?

Only when necessary, and only with the right rigging and precautions — we'll explain exactly what the job involves before we start.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily, but we recommend it for the initial walkthrough so we can point out anything you should know about the tree or the property.

What if it rains on the scheduled day?

We'll call to reschedule — safety comes first, especially with larger removals.
