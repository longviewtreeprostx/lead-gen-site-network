Documentation

Commercial and HOA work often comes with insurance and liability documentation requirements that a typical residential job doesn't. Let us know what your property or board requires up front and we'll make sure it's in place before the crew shows up, not after.

Why proactive tree management matters for liability

For a property manager or HOA board, a tree issue is rarely just a maintenance question — it's a liability question. A dead limb over a parking lot, a leaning tree near a shared walkway, or overgrowth blocking a security camera's sightline all carry real exposure if something happens before it's addressed. A standing maintenance relationship means hazard trees get flagged and handled on a schedule, rather than only after a resident complaint, an insurance inspection, or an incident forces the issue.

What a recurring maintenance schedule typically covers

Every property is different, but a standing arrangement usually includes a walkthrough on a set cycle (often annually or semi-annually) to flag developing hazards before they're urgent, scheduled trimming for common-area or boundary trees, priority response for storm damage ahead of one-off residential calls, and a single point of contact for scheduling and invoicing instead of re-explaining the property every time something comes up. We'll scope the specific cadence and coverage to your property size and needs rather than a one-size-fits-all package.
