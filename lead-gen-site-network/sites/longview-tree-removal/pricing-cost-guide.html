Every tree removal site quotes "starting at" prices that rarely reflect what you'll actually pay. We'd rather tell you the real range and what pushes a job toward the low or high end of it, so a quote from us isn't a surprise.

Typical price ranges

Small tree (under 30 ft)

Roughly $150–$500. Ornamental trees, young trees, or anything a single crew member could largely handle without heavy equipment.

Medium tree (30–60 ft)

Roughly $500–$1,200. Most mature residential shade trees — oaks, pines, sweetgums — fall in this range when access is reasonable and there's nothing complicating the drop zone.

Large or hazardous tree (60+ ft, or near structures/lines)

Roughly $1,200–$2,500+. Large mature pines and oaks, trees that have to be taken down in sections, or anything close enough to a house, fence, or power line that it needs careful rigging instead of a straight drop.

What actually moves the price

Size is the obvious factor, but it's rarely the only one that matters. Accessibility counts for a lot — a tree a truck and chipper can pull right up to costs less to remove than one behind a fence, over a septic field, or wedged between a shed and a property line where everything has to be carried out by hand. Hazard level matters too: a tree leaning toward a house or tangled in power lines takes more time, more careful rigging, and sometimes a second crew member just for safety, and that shows up in the price. Disposal is the other piece — hauling everything away costs more than leaving cut rounds and mulched brush on-site if you don't mind keeping the wood or having it mulched into your yard.

Why we don't quote an exact price online

We could give you a lowball "starting at $199" number to get the phone to ring, but it wouldn't hold up once we saw the tree — and that's a bad way to start a job. A quick on-site look (or in some cases detailed photos and a phone walkthrough) lets us give you a number we'll actually stand behind, not one that grows once the crew shows up.

Frequently asked questions

How much does tree removal cost in Longview, TX?

Most residential removals run $300–$2,000 depending on size, access, and hazard level. Larger or more complicated removals can run higher. A free on-site quote is the only way to get an exact number.

Why can't I just get a price over the phone?

Trunk diameter, lean direction, canopy tangles with other trees, proximity to structures or lines, and debris access all affect price, and none of those are reliable to judge from a photo or a phone description alone.

Does stump grinding cost extra?

Yes, unless bundled in. It's usually priced separately, with a better combined rate when done in the same visit as the tree removal.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover tree removal?

Sometimes — commonly covered when a fallen tree damaged a covered structure, often not covered for pre-emptive removal or a fallen tree that caused no damage. Check your specific policy.
