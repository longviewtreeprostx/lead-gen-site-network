Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Longview?

Requirements can depend on your specific property, the tree, and the situation. Give us a call and we'll help you figure out what, if anything, applies before any work begins.

How much does tree removal cost?

Cost depends mainly on the tree's size and species, how accessible it is, and whether it's a routine or hazardous/storm situation. We'll walk the property with you and give you a firm price before we start.

What happens to the wood and debris after removal?

We haul it away as part of standard service. If you'd like to keep firewood-length rounds, just ask — we're happy to leave them.
